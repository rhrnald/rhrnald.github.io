About

I am a systems researcher focused on high-performance GPU computing and numerical algorithms. I study performance problems in complex computing systems by structuring them mathematically, analyzing bottlenecks across the hardware-software boundary, and validating ideas through real implementations and experiments. My work is guided by mathematical reasoning and algorithmic problem-solving, with an emphasis on FFTs, sparse solvers, and accelerator-optimized computation.

Recently, I have worked on hardware-aware optimization for modern GPUs, including Tensor Core FFT kernels, sparse direct solvers for heterogeneous systems, and data-movement-efficient methods that improve locality, utilization, and predictable performance.
