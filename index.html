Research Summary

I am a systems researcher focused on high-performance GPU computing and numerical algorithms. I am motivated by mathematical reasoning and algorithmic problem-solving, which guide my work on FFTs, sparse solvers, and accelerator-optimized computation. My research aims to develop hardware-aware methods that reduce data movement, improve locality, and deliver predictable high utilization on modern accelerators.

GPU systems FFT and numerical algorithms Sparse computation Compiler/runtime optimization Multi-GPU systems
  • High-performance GPU and accelerator systems; parallel and heterogeneous computing
  • Algorithm-hardware co-design; compiler IR, kernel generation, and runtime optimization
  • Sparse computation and sparsity-driven algorithms
  • Numerical algorithms (FFT, linear algebra) and data-movement-efficient computation
  • Distributed and multi-GPU systems; communication-efficient parallel algorithms
